## What it does

- 🎵 Play any song by name: “play believer”, “play believer by imagine dragons”
- ⏭️ “Next song”, “previous song”, “pause”, “resume”
- 🔊 “Increase volume” / “decrease volume” — one real hardware step
- 📵 Keeps listening with the screen locked
- 🧠 Speech recognition runs **on the phone** (Whisper) — no voice is uploaded
- 🎧 Works with Bluetooth, wired earphones or the phone speaker

No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics. It's a student project, not a product.

## Before you install

- ✅ **Android 7 or newer** (iPhone is not supported yet)
- ✅ **Spotify Premium** — Spotify blocks third-party control for Free accounts
- ✅ Allow **“install from unknown sources”** when Android asks (direct APK, not Play Store)
- ✅ **Earphones / headphones** strongly recommended; with the phone speaker the mic hears the song instead of you
- ✅ **One-time ~140 MB model download** in Settings → Speech recognition (then recognition works offline)
- ✅ Internet for Spotify

## How to use (1 minute)

1. Open the app → **Connect Spotify** → approve.
2. Settings → **Speech recognition** → **Download** (once).
3. Tap **Ride Mode**.
4. Say **“play believer”**, **“next song”**, **“pause”**.
5. Tap **“Allow background running”** once, then lock the screen — it keeps listening.

You don't have to ride a bike to test it — walking, commuting or sitting with earphones works the same.

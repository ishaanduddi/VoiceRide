- 🎧 Any output works: helmet comms, Bluetooth earbuds, wired earphones, phone speaker

## Before you install — the fine print (important)

- ✅ **Android phone** (Android 7 or newer)
- ✅ **Spotify Premium** — Spotify blocks playback control for Free accounts, so the app can't work without it
- ✅ **Allow “install from unknown sources”** when Android asks — this is a direct APK, not a Play Store listing
- ✅ **Headphones / earbuds / helmet comms** strongly recommended — with the phone speaker, the mic hears the song instead of you
- ✅ **One-time speech model download** (~140 MB) in Settings → Speech recognition. After that, recognition works offline
- ✅ **Internet** for Spotify itself

## How to use it (60 seconds)

1. Open the app → **Connect Spotify** → approve.
2. Settings → **Speech recognition** → **Download** the model (once).
3. Optional: pick a playlist. Totally optional — you can play any song by name.
4. Tap **Ride Mode** and start.
5. Say: **“play believer”**, **“next song”**, **“pause”**, **“increase volume”**.
6. Lock the screen and keep riding. Tap **“Allow background running”** once so Android doesn't freeze it.

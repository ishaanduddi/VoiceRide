# VoiceRiders

Hands-free Spotify control for riders. Say **“play believer”**, **“next song”** or
**“set volume to 70”** and keep your hands on the bars.

- **Android only** (beta, Android 13–16 tested)
- Requires **Spotify Premium**
- Speech recognition runs **on the phone** (Whisper) — no voice is uploaded
